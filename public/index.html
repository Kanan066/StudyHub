Questions

Is StudyHub free?

Yes. It is a student project, free to use.

Yes. The layout adapts to phones, tablets and laptops.

Is my data private?

Passwords are hashed and every task, note and session is tied to your account only.

Does it have dark mode?

Yes, switch it from the dashboard. Your choice is remembered.
